## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a conceptual editorial specimen study and poetic visual taxonomy. Show only part of the subject as highly detailed monochrome photography. Reconstruct the missing portion with large untouched negative space and a fragile imperfect hand-drawn contour. Treat the object as both matter and idea. Sparse sensory words annotate intangible qualities rather than literal anatomy. A few isolated square texture samples act as material evidence. Extreme whitespace, restrained asymmetry, small bold lowercase grotesk, a thin marginal rule, tactile black-and-white grain and subtle analog print imperfections. Quiet, cerebral, tender, archival. It is not a full-colour illustration, a filled catalogue grid, a cute sticker sheet, a smooth vector diagram or a loud infographic.
